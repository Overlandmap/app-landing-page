About Overland Map

The Mission

Overland Map is dedicated to providing overlanders with accurate, up-to-date information about border crossings, visa requirements, vehicle documentation, and travel conditions around the world. We believe that adventure should be accessible to everyone, and that reliable information is key to safe and successful overland travel.

What You Will Find

  • •Interactive maps showing where it's easy or difficult to travel
  • •Detailed information about carnet requirements and vehicle paperwork
  • •Climate maps to help you plan the best time to travel
  • •Curated itineraries in selected destinations
  • •Community-driven updates on border crossings and road conditions

Community Driven

Our data is continuously updated by the overlanding community. Through our mobile app, travelers can share their experiences, report border crossing conditions, and help keep information current for fellow adventurers. This collaborative approach ensures that our maps reflect real-world conditions, not just official regulations.

Get Involved

Download our mobile app to contribute your experiences, access detailed itineraries, and connect with other overlanders. Together, we're building the most comprehensive resource for overland travel worldwide.
